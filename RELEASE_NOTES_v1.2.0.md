# Version 1.2.0

This release contains the reproducibility materials for the manuscript **Therapy-associated shifts in lineage-derived RNA can shape apparent whole-blood treatment signatures**, intended for submission to the *Journal of Translational Medicine*.

The release adds or documents:

- the recalculated platelet RNA-score specificity model with classical and patient-clustered CR1 sensitivity estimates;
- the exact GSE112278 transformation and analysis environments;
- selection-independent genome-wide, recent-IVIG, rank-based, and cross-lineage analyses;
- composition-only mixture experiments with donor fixed effects and donor-stratified null-label calibration;
- updated supplementary methods, results, figures, source-data tables, and reproducibility documentation;
- the distinction between RNA-abundance proxies and measured cell counts or fractions;
- the distinction between held-out transcriptome reconstruction and clinical-response prediction;
- the limitation that cross-lineage transport analyses are not clinical replications of the primary eltrombopag analysis.
