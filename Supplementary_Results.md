# Supplementary Results

## Signature robustness and patient exclusion

Leave-one-marker-out platelet scores remained highly correlated with the fixed 12-gene score (r=0.997–1.000). Responder score changes remained positive (0.857–0.926), nonresponder changes remained negative (−0.400 to −0.346), and exact between-group P values ranged from 0.0329 to 0.0483. Across 15 leave-one-patient-out analyses, responder score changes ranged from 0.671 to 0.976, median top-208 coupling ranged from 0.863 to 0.935, and attenuation ranged from 60.1% to 91.4%.

## Month-1 and young-platelet sensitivity analyses

Among complete cases, responder month-1 versus baseline score change was 0.496 (n=9; exact P=0.00781), while the nonresponder change was 0.002 (n=5; P=1.00). The responder week-1 to month-1 change was −0.192 (n=8; P=0.0859). Abundance-adjusted young-minus-mature platelet residuals were not significant across the 25-, 50-, or 100-gene definitions; responder P values ranged from 0.404 to 0.650.

## Cross-lineage specificity

The erythroid score increased after erythropoietin (change 1.187; n=10; exact P=0.001953; partial R2=0.356). The B-cell score decreased after rituximab relative to placebo (difference −1.912; active n=25, placebo n=10; permutation P=0.00001; partial R2=0.198). Fully nested held-out R2 values were 0.066 and 0.158, respectively.

## Composition-only benchmark calibration

Across 100 donor-adjusted composition-only experiments, the B-cell score correlated with the known B-cell fraction at median r=0.967. Conventional testing produced 3,037 FDR-significant genes at the median, whereas B-cell-score adjustment produced 0 (2.5th–97.5th percentile, 0–2). The donor-stratified null-label calibration produced 0 discoveries at the median (0–1), supporting type-I-error calibration under no group–composition association.

## Immunometabolic audit

Platelet adjustment attenuated apparent whole-blood lipid-pathway effects. Purified T-cell and donor-level bone-marrow datasets did not constitute longitudinal eltrombopag replication and are therefore reported as secondary interpretive audits only.
